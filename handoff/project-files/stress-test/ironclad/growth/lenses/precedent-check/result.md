## Blockers

- **[critical] scope**: The paid gate cannot be decided as written, and it has no branch for "never passes".
  - Not enumerated: the doc says "all five checks hold" (`source-growth.md:351`) but lists four (`:32-35`: edge in the live record, 2,000 followers, lawyer's review, licensed prices). The fifth, "8+ weeks live inside the backtest range", exists only in `notes/growth-plan.md:12`. "The first two checks make sure we sell something real" (`:178`) names no checks.
  - "Edge" is undefined. The engine defines a backtest edge (beats placebo and keyword baseline after 5 and 20 bp costs, Benjamini-Hochberg across all cells; `engine.md:161-164`). The growth plan never says which segment, window, effect size or interval must pass. With 8-12 topics, many tickers and seven result windows (`engine.md:150,158`) there are hundreds of topic x ticker x window cells, so "an edge somewhere" will often appear by chance and is not a thing to sell.
  - Eight live weeks cannot confirm an edge. My rough arithmetic: `post-timing/README.md` gives 21,862 text posts over about 4.6 years, roughly 13 a day, so about 700 posts in 8 weeks. If 10-20% name an instrument (an assumption), that is 70-140 alerts. Detecting a 60% vs 50% hit rate at 5% one-sided and 80% power needs about 150 independent cases, and the engine resamples by trading day, so effective n is lower still. After multiple-testing correction the live window can reject disaster but not confirm a modest edge. As written the gate returns "inconclusive" and says nothing about what then.
  - 2,000 "followers" (Telegram plus X) double-counts people, is cheap to inflate on X, and says nothing about willingness to pay.
  - Order: the two cheap, binary, product-shaping checks (securities-lawyer scoping, Alpaca's written consent) come last, after 8 weeks and 2,000 followers. A lawyer "no" on Option A, or Alpaca "no" on commercial use, invalidates the paid tier after all that time was spent.
  - Never-passes: only "no edge" is handled (`:166-168`, "rethought together with the engine"). Nothing covers followers stalling, counsel rejecting the structure, Alpaca refusing, or the running cost of a free product. Precedent: the same project wrote this kind of gate before. `ROADMAP.md:1012-1013` set "Revenue >$150/month, 10+ paying subscribers" for Phase 3, and `:997,:1003` set "Accuracy >60%" and "Alert hit rate >65%" under Phases 0 and 1, which are headed COMPLETE (`:5` last updated 2026-02-13). Phase 3 has sat PENDING since February with no subscriber count recorded. Gates in this repo have been stated and never evaluated against data.

    Add a time-box: if the gate has not passed 90 days after public launch, Chris picks one of three pre-agreed outcomes: (a) stay free as a research and portfolio project (the project's origin, "portfolio demo", `ROADMAP.md:3` and `CHANGELOG.md:1193,1229`; `ROADMAP.md:906` research publication), (b) pull Step 3 (API for builders) forward, (c) wind down the outlets and the always-on cost.
