## Blockers

- **[critical] scope**: The paid gate cannot be tested as written, and its steps are circular. Doc evidence: "What you decide", row 1, lists four conditions (live-record edge, 2,000 followers, lawyer's review, licensed prices). "Jobs for you" says "only if all five checks hold". "Money > The gate" says "the first two checks make sure we sell something real" without naming them. The planner note's five include "8+ weeks live inside backtest range", which the exported prose never states (it may exist only in the gate diagram, shown as a placeholder). Specific defects:
  - **Not measurable.** "An edge" does not say which layer (live, keyword-over-history, LLM backfill), which cost level (the engine reports 5 and 20 bp), or which test. The engine's own rule is Wilson intervals, Benjamini-Hochberg across every topic x ticker x window, and a placebo. "2,000 followers" does not say Telegram or X, although Whop gates Telegram only, the two overlap, and "What it could earn" uses followers as the paying base. "Licensed prices" does not say what is being licensed or what it may cost.
  - **Not reachable in the time given.** At the notification plan's own assumption of 2 to 5 sent alerts a day, 8 weeks gives roughly 110 to 280 alerts, clustered by day. Detecting a 60% hit rate against a 50% placebo at 95% confidence and 80% power needs about 150 to 190 independent cases (my arithmetic), and the engine marks anything under 20 cases LOW and corrects across all cells. A per-cell edge will essentially never show in 8 weeks. Only one pre-registered, pooled test can pass.
  - **Wrong order.** The lawyer's review is a gate condition, yet "At the gate, only if all five checks hold. I bring you the lawyer's cost" and "After the lawyer's review: open a Whop account" make it something bought after the gate passes. As written it can never be the fifth check.
  - **No "never passes" branch and no dates.** Nothing says what happens if any condition fails or stalls. The earliest paid day is cutover (engine PR 8 of 12) + 8 weeks + lawyer lead time, and the doc gives no date, so Chris cannot judge whether "find a way to make $" is a quarter or a year away.
  - **Recommendation:** Replace the prose with a numbered gate Chris ratifies, each line with a threshold, a source and a date check. Proposed defaults, all Chris's to change: G1 launch edge (needed for the public launch, from the engine's PR 9 report): pooled abnormal return after 20 bp costs above zero, interval excluding zero, BH-corrected against the placebo, with the layer named. G2 live confirmation: at least 8 weeks and about 150 live alerts since cutover, pooled live result not below zero and consistent with G1's interval. G3 audience: at least 1,000 Telegram channel members read by hand from Telegram (X followers recorded, not counted; 1,000 is also Telegram's ad-share line). G4 lawyer: a short scoping call right after G1 asking one question (can event-timed alerts be sold at all, see the legal finding), then the full review only after G1 to G3 pass. G5 display rights: ask Alpaca in writing now whether commercial use of derived percentage moves is allowed; if yes, G5 shrinks to "only needed for raw prices or charts" and leaves the paid tier. Then add one line per failure: G1 fails means no public launch and the null result goes out as research; G2 fails after 16 weeks means stay free; G3 flat at day 90 after launch means a review that offers stay free at running cost only or wind down; G4 says no means the fallback revenue list in the legal finding; G5 unobtainable means the tier sells no price display. Add an "earliest possible paid date" line.
