## The failure story
The mirrors deliver at a median of 6 to 9 minutes: CNN refreshes every 8 to 26 minutes, and trumpstruth's origin runs 3 to 17 minutes behind. Firms on TMTG's millisecond feed, and headline accounts, move prices within seconds. By the time the engine alerts, the 5-, 15- and 60-minute windows are priced in. The longer windows (close, 1, 3 and 5 days) are dominated by everything else that happens. After BH across hundreds of topic×ticker×window cells and 20 bp costs, nothing survives. That verdict lands at PR 10. By then the team has shipped the engine, two WebSocket streams, per-minute progress, crypto support, N1, N2, D1 and a public channel whose record is weeks of unevidenced calls. 'Stop and rethink' comes after about 80% of the effort, and the growth plan has nothing to launch.
