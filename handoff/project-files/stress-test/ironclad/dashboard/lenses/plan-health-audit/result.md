## Blockers

- **[critical] compatibility**: D1 builds a public raw-price surface that the engine, notification and growth plans have ruled out. The Signal page chart is "1-minute prices since the post with SPY beside them" (Pages > Signal page, item 4), it is served from `/api/v1/bars/<symbol>` (What the dashboard reads; D1 writes it), and the Evidence drill-down lists "entry price" per post (Pages > Evidence).   - **Recommendation:** Rewrite D1's chart and Evidence contracts before any build. (1) No public endpoint returns a price, a bar or an entry price. Replace `/bars/<symbol>` with an endpoint that returns two % series (ticker and benchmark since the post, computed server-side from `market_bars` and `alert_progress`) plus the event marks. (2) Delete "entry price" from the Evidence drill-down. (3) Say that D1 owns the check of `price_sources.display_rights` that the engine plan says "the API" performs. (4) Add a D1 test that walks every public response schema and fails on any price-like field. (5) Ask the engine and notification owners to strip entry price from the public alert.v1 or confirm it stays private.
