# Contrarian (10th Man): argue the plan fails

Run because the inline path requires it regardless of the Blocker count (the five angles above shared one session and one set of priors).

## Mandate: at least one Blocker

**Blocker: the dashboard is being built in full before anyone knows whether there is anything to show, and the cutover waits for it.**

- Every headline feature (tier, typical move, "how much is left", results per window, Evidence, Track record) arrives with engine PR 10, and PR 10 is gated on an edge that may not exist. The engine plan's own biggest risk is "a free feed is never first", and trading firms buy TMTG's millisecond feed. The prior that a 2-4 minute-late public alert on Trump posts survives 5 to 20 bp costs is low.
- Meanwhile D1, now carrying growth's ten items (domain, canonical links, sitemap, server-rendered titles and preview images for five page types, ticker, topic and report pages, receipts, follow buttons with ?ref, visit counts, methodology and disclaimer, % charts, README), is on the engine's critical path: "the cutover waits for it".
- Reference class: today's React feed, one page, took 16 commits over three weeks (git log -- frontend/, 2026-03-25 to 2026-04-14), and it replaced an earlier Dash UI (a537bc4, "remove old Dash UI"). This would be the project's third dashboard, at roughly four to six times that scope, gating a cutover.
- Failure story: the engine's shadow week is clean, the cutover slips two weeks waiting for D1, ScrapeCreators and twelve old services keep billing, PR 10 shows no edge, and the polished signal page shows a call, a % line and "evidence is still being built" indefinitely.

**Contrarian's fix:** at the cutover ship only what needs no edge (a home page, the signal page, a record page with live grades, an About page) and nothing else; build Evidence, report, ticker and topic pages only after PR 10 shows an edge. If there is no edge, the record site is still honest and useful, and nothing else was built.

## Second objection (not a Blocker)

Public by default plus "is it still worth trading?" plus per-ticker pages plus a soft launch at cutover puts the most advice-like artifact in the project in public before the lawyer, who the growth plan only consults at the paid gate. The growth plan's own case law note (SEC v. Park: a $29 email alert service not a bona fide publication) is about paid alerts, but the wording habit set now carries into the paid tier.

## Synthesis outcome

Partly accepted. The cut list (fewer pages, no D2/D4, growth items scoped down) and "nothing gated on the edge is built before the edge" go into the result. Rejected: deferring the site entirely, because today's site goes stale at the cutover and growth's soft launch needs a page to link to. The cutover coupling is kept as a major Risk rather than a Blocker, because the fix is sequencing (decouple D1 from the cutover sitting), not redesign.
